Malineni Lakshmaiah Women's Engineering College

Learn. Innovate. Lead.

Malineni Lakshmaiah Women's Engineering College provides students with an environment where they can develop technical knowledge, practical skills and professional confidence.

The college focuses on engineering education, practical learning, innovation and preparing students for opportunities in the technology sector.

  • Technical and practical learning
  • Student-centered education
  • Innovation and project development
  • Career and placement preparation
  • Extra-curricular activities
